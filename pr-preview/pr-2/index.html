05FAQ

What do you actually take on?
LLM routing, agent loops, local developer tools, and authorized security work. If the job is a slide deck or a growth hack, I will pass.
Are you a freelancer or a founder?
Both. I am building warm.run and routing.run. Consulting is for problems that are sharp enough to be worth the context switch.
Do you log prompts on routing.run?
No. Zero prompt logging is the product. If a vendor needs your prompts to improve the model, that is their business, not yours.
Where are you based?
Kashmir. I ship from the mountains. Timezone is IST. Async email is the default.
How should I email you?
Write to zentex@warm.run. Put the problem in the first paragraph. Links beat PDFs.
Do you only do authorized security work?
Yes. Scope in writing. I do not hunt friends, family, or random production boxes. If you need that energy, look elsewhere.
Can you work inside an existing repo?
Yes. That is the usual case. I read the codebase, I do not replace it with a rewrite unless the rewrite is the actual job.
What does this cost?
It depends on the blast radius. I quote in writing after I understand the problem. If the quote is wrong for you, we stop there. No retainers hiding in the weeds.
