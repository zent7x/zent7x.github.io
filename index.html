About Work Writing Elsewhere

I’m Adeeb, a founder and security researcher in Kashmir.

I’m building warm.run, where coding agents work through the night and leave PRs to review, and routing.run, an LLM gateway that doesn’t log your prompts.

I also make open-source tools: terminal-fenster puts a real browser in your terminal; grasp gives agents the code they need; cogrep searches code locally.

Most of my work is in Rust and TypeScript. I like building tools that are small enough to understand and useful without an internet connection.

My security work includes an engagement with Publive, where I found and reported critical bugs.
